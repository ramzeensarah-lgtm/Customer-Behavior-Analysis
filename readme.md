# Alfido Tech Revenue Optimization Analysis

## Objective

Analyze sales data to identify revenue drivers and provide actionable business recommendations.

## Business Question

Which regions, categories, and products generate the highest revenue, and how can business performance be improved?

## Tools Used

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Google Colab
* GitHub

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

## Key Findings

* Total Revenue: $2.26 Million
* Total Orders: 4,922
* Total Customers: 793
* West region generated the highest revenue.
* Technology was the highest-performing category.
* Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the top-selling product.
* Peak sales occurred during September, November, and December.

## Recommendations

1. Focus on high-performing regions.
2. Increase inventory for technology products.
3. Promote top-selling products.
4. Improve performance in lower-performing regions.
5. Leverage seasonal demand through targeted campaigns.

## Conclusion

The analysis identified key revenue drivers and seasonal trends that can support better business decisions and long-term growth.
